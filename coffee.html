A WebDev's Path

And this is my journey to learn web development:

How the Idea came Up

I've always wanted to be creative but could never do it. I wanted to get into arts but didn't have the talent, then I wanted to write but didn't keep it up.

Web dev was always attractive but a bit out of my reach because of time and money. Art fascinates me just as much as working with my hands. This way I feel I can create something as well as be inspired.

Why now?

It gets interesting here. Or maybe not... I got the idea of trying again after I met my soulmate, I want to start settling my life properly and I need to have a job I enjoy and also gets wayed well. If you do something you enjoy you never work a day in your life, right?

What I needed was this...

  1. A reason to.
  2. Motivation.
  3. The brains for it.

I have all them so I got to work. I just needed someone to get my out of my rut and lift me up. I found that someone and now I'm getting greedy and doing it for myself :)

Here's how I'm doing it:

I started out like most people with "Hello World!" but that gets old pretty fast so I though of something else. I remebered that Google is my friend and started looking for things. Youtube I great and all that, it teaches you stuff but eventually falls flat. So I thought to myself, why not use every resource at my disposal. I looked for tutorials on YT, asked ChatGpt, googled stuff and then trial and error, that's the best one. Now practice makes perfect.

Thanks for reading my journey.

Still learning. Still building. Still improving.
